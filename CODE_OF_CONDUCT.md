# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in Earnexx welcoming, friendly, and harassment-free for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language  
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences  
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism  
- Focusing on what is best for the community  
- Showing empathy towards other community members  

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form  
- Trolling or insulting/derogatory comments  
- Public or private attacks on other contributors  
- Any other behavior that harms the community  

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project lead:

- **Email:** bullsniperr@gmail.com  
- **X/Twitter DM:** @BullSniperr

All reports will be reviewed and responded to promptly.
